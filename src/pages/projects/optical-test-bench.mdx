## The challenge

The project required an automated bench capable of supporting digital defect-detection measurements with substantially better accuracy, repeatability, and operational consistency than the preceding setup. The mechanical system had to connect the measurement geometry, sensor positioning, calibration method, test access, and production use case.

Because this was an industrial project, the public case study does not reproduce the equipment geometry, components, drawings, software, data, or internal requirements.

## My contribution

I led the mechanical-design work and drove project delivery from requirements through build, testing, demonstration, and transfer. My work included:

- translating the measurement need into mechanical and verification requirements;
- developing the test-bench architecture and detailed design in SolidWorks;
- supporting prototype builds and the integration of the measurement setup;
- defining test protocols and acceptance criteria;
- coordinating R&D, production, and suppliers around builds and demonstrations;
- documenting the evidence needed for reuse and production transfer.

## Engineering approach

### Design around the measurement chain

The mechanical architecture was treated as part of the measurement system rather than as a passive support. Design decisions were evaluated against positioning repeatability, geometric stability, calibration access, test execution, and maintainability.

### Make acceptance measurable

Requirements were connected to explicit protocols and acceptance criteria. This created a traceable route from the intended function to the evidence needed at testing and handover.

### Use operating data to improve availability

SPC control charts and observed process behaviour were used to refine calibration intervals. This connected metrology performance to equipment availability instead of treating calibration as a fixed administrative schedule.

## Outcomes

| Outcome | Public evidence |
| --- | --- |
| Measurement performance | **±5 µm achieved accuracy**, a **10× improvement** |
| Equipment availability | **15% reduction in downtime** through SPC and calibration-interval optimization |
| Delivery | Protocols, acceptance criteria, documented results, demonstrations, and production-transfer support |

## What this demonstrates

This project shows the ability to join mechanical design, calibration, experimental validation, data analysis, stakeholder coordination, and operational improvement around one measurable system objective.

> The value was not only a more precise bench. It was a measurement process that could be built, tested, explained, and transferred with evidence.
